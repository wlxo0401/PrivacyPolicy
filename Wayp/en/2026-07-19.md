# Privacy Policy — Wayp

**Effective date:** 19 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how personal data is processed in the mobile application **"Wayp"** (the "App"), developed by Jitae Kim ("we", "us"). Wayp is a speedometer and trip-recording app that uses GPS to measure and display your driving speed and route in real time and to keep a history of your trips. This policy is written to satisfy the requirements of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as a global baseline, with a dedicated section for California residents (CCPA/CPRA).

**Summary:** All data the App processes — location, trip records and settings — is processed and stored **only on your device**. There is no account, no server transmission, and no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK. We (the developer) **do not collect, transmit or share any of your data** with any third party.

## 2. Categories of Personal Data

The App does not require an account. Everything below is created and kept **only on your device**.

### 2-1. Location data (stored on-device only)

- GPS coordinates, travel route, speed, and heading

The App uses your device's GPS to display your driving speed and route in real time and to record your trips. **While a recording is in progress, location continues to be collected even when the screen is off or the App is in the background** (background location updates). Location data is processed and stored **only on your device** and is **never transmitted to any server operated by us or a third party**.

### 2-2. Data you generate (stored on-device only)

- **Trip records:** trip date/time, route, speed graph, travel mode
- **App settings:** speed unit (km/h or mph), theme, keep-screen-on preference

Trip records are stored in **on-device SwiftData** and settings in on-device UserDefaults. iCloud (CloudKit) synchronization is not used, so this data never leaves your device.

### 2-3. Data collected automatically

The App uses **no** analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK, and therefore does **not** automatically collect any information that identifies or tracks you. The App contains no networking code that transmits data off the device. The only exception is that, when a map is shown, Apple Maps (MapKit) may contact Apple's servers to load map tiles; this is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and we collect no data in that process.

## 7. Your Rights under GDPR (Arts. 15–22)

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and to object to processing, as well as to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Because all data is stored **only on your device and we hold no copy**, you exercise these rights directly:
- **Access / rectification / erasure:** view and delete trip records within the App; uninstalling the App erases all on-device data.
- **Withdraw location permission:** iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Wayp.

**Supervisory authorities.** If you reside in the United Kingdom, your supervisory authority is the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** — https://ico.org.uk/. If you reside in the EU or EEA, you may contact the data protection authority of your country of residence; the European Data Protection Board lists the national authorities at https://edpb.europa.eu/. To exercise any right or raise a question, contact **wlxo0401@gmail.com**.
